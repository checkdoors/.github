# Security Policy

Checkdoors takes the security of its platform and its users' data seriously.
This policy applies to all repositories in the Checkdoors organization.

## Reporting a vulnerability

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability in any Checkdoors
system, please report it responsibly. **Do not open a public issue** and do not
disclose the details publicly until we have had a reasonable opportunity to
investigate and respond.

Report security concerns through the contact channels listed at
[checkdoors.com](https://checkdoors.com). Please include:

- A description of the issue and its potential impact
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept
- Any relevant logs, requests, or screenshots
- Affected components, URLs, or versions if known

## What to expect

- We will acknowledge your report as we review it.
- We will keep you informed as we investigate and work toward a fix.
- We ask that you give us a reasonable window to remediate before any public
  disclosure.

## Scope

This policy covers Checkdoors-operated services and the repositories in this
organization. Third-party services Checkdoors relies on are governed by their
own security policies; please report issues in those systems to the respective
provider.

## Good-faith research

We will not pursue action against researchers who report vulnerabilities in
good faith, avoid privacy violations and service disruption, and do not access
or modify data beyond what is necessary to demonstrate the issue.
